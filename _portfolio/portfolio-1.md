Water Accounting Plus (WA+) Framework is an advanced version of the IWMI and developed by IHE-Delft and FAO that combines open-source remote sensing datasets with the measurement of ground truth data to generate standardized results in a different Sheets along with the tables, Maps and graphs. This WA+ framework has been designed and developed to disseminate water related statistics that can be understand by the water managers and policy makers ('/images/GIS2.png')
In my Ph.D. I used WA+ framework in a semi-arid catchment (Central Godavari Basin) of India to assess water resources and estimate the water productivity/land productivity for sustaining food security in context of the climate change and human’s interventions
