I am dedicated researcher in the geospatial field, with over five years of research and professional experience with a PhD in Geoinformatics. My research focuses on water resources, ecological drought, remote sensing, and spatial analysis using ArcGIS Pro, Python, and Google Earth Engine. My PhD dissertation, "Developing a Satellite-based Water Accounting Plus (WA+) Framework in the Godavari River Basin under Changing Climate", focused on integrated geospatial analysis to quantifying water–ecosystem interactions and their implications for the water–food–energy nexus under climate change. My research interests span ecosystem services, water resource management, food and water security, and climate change. Methodologically, I specialize in Remote Sensing and GIS, the WA+ framework, Budyko-based hydrological modeling, and climate extremes analysis. I am currently seeking GIS Analyst and Geospatial Data Scientist opportunities in the United States.
